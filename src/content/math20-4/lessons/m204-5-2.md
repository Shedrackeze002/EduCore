You know you earned \$612.50 this pay period at \$17.50 an hour. How many hours were you paid for? To answer, you use the pay formula **backwards** by solving an equation.

## Equations and inverse operations: a quick review

:::definition Equation
An **equation** is a math sentence with an equal sign, such as $x + 7 = 15$. To **solve** it means to find the number that makes it true.
:::

:::definition Inverse operation
An **inverse operation** undoes another operation. Adding and subtracting undo each other. Multiplying and dividing undo each other.
:::

:::figure

:::

An equation is like a balance. Whatever you do to one side, do the same to the other side.

:::example One step: adding
Solve $x + 7 = 15$.
@step To undo $+\ 7$, subtract $7$ from both sides: $x + 7 - 7 = 15 - 7$
@step $x = 8$
@answer $x = 8$. Check: $8 + 7 = 15$.
:::

:::example One step: multiplying
Solve $4n = 36$.
@step $4n$ means $4 \times n$. To undo $\times\ 4$, divide both sides by $4$.
@step $n = 36 \div 4 = 9$
@answer $n = 9$. Check: $4 \times 9 = 36$.
:::

## Two-step equations

Now one step further. When an equation has two operations, undo them in **reverse** order. Undo adding or subtracting first. Then undo multiplying or dividing.

:::example A two-step equation
Solve $3x + 5 = 26$.
@step Undo the $+\ 5$: subtract $5$ from both sides: $3x = 21$
@step Undo the $\times\ 3$: divide both sides by $3$: $x = 7$
@answer $x = 7$. Check: $3 \times 7 + 5 = 26$.
:::

:::example Bowling
Bowling costs \$5 for shoes plus \$6 per game. Tyler paid \$29. Solve $5 + 6g = 29$ to find how many games he played.
@step Subtract $5$ from both sides: $6g = 24$
@step Divide both sides by $6$: $g = 4$
@answer Tyler played $4$ games. Check: $5 + 6 \times 4 = 29$.
:::

:::mistake Dividing first
In $3x + 5 = 26$, students often divide $3x$ and $26$ by $3$ but forget the $5$. Then the answer comes out wrong. Undo the adding or subtracting **first**, and the division is easy.
:::

:::

## Using a formula backwards

When you know the answer of a formula but not one of its inputs, substitute what you know. Then solve for the missing value.

:::example How many hours?
Use $E = rh$. You earned \$612.50 at \$17.50 per hour. How many hours did you work?
@step Substitute: $612.50 = 17.50h$
@step Divide both sides by $17.50$: $h = 612.50 \div 17.50 = 35$
@answer You worked $35$ hours.
:::

:::example How far was the taxi ride?
A taxi charges $C = 4 + 2.5d$. A ride cost \$31.50. How many kilometres was the ride?
@step Substitute: $31.50 = 4 + 2.5d$
@step Subtract $4$ from both sides: $27.50 = 2.5d$
@step Divide both sides by $2.5$: $d = 11$
@answer The ride was $11\ \text{km}$.
:::

:::example From Fahrenheit to Celsius
Use $F = 1.8C + 32$. A website says it is $50^\circ\text{F}$. What is that in degrees Celsius?
@step Substitute: $50 = 1.8C + 32$
@step Subtract $32$ from both sides: $18 = 1.8C$
@step Divide both sides by $1.8$: $C = 10$
@answer It is $10^\circ\text{C}$.
:::

:::

## Checking your answer

Always put your answer back into the equation. If both sides match, your answer is right.

:::example Catching a mistake
Noor solved $2w + 30 = 46$ and got $w = 38$. Is she right?
@step Check: $2 \times 38 + 30 = 106$, not $46$. Something went wrong.
@step Redo it: subtract $30$ from both sides: $2w = 16$. Divide by $2$: $w = 8$
@step Check: $2 \times 8 + 30 = 46$. It works.
@answer The correct answer is $w = 8$. Noor added $30$ instead of subtracting it.
:::

:::tip Does the answer make sense?
A taxi ride of $500\ \text{km}$ or a shift of $90$ hours should make you stop and check your work.
:::

:::check m204-solve-equation 3
:::

:::summary
- To solve an equation, use inverse operations. Do the same thing to both sides.
- For two-step equations, undo adding or subtracting first, then undo multiplying or dividing.
- To use a formula backwards, substitute what you know, then solve for the missing letter.
- Check by putting your answer back into the equation.
:::
