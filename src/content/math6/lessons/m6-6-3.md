A class trip costs \$50 for the bus plus \$4 for each student. The total is \$102. How many students are going? An equation can answer this question. In this lesson, you will simplify equations and solve them.

## Equations and solutions

:::definition Equation
An **equation** says that two expressions are equal, such as $n + 7 = 15$. The **solution** is the value of the variable that makes it true.
:::

To solve an equation, use **inverse operations** to get the variable by itself. Adding and subtracting undo each other. Multiplying and dividing undo each other. Whatever you do to one side, do to the other side too.

:::example One operation
Solve $4x = 28$.
@step $4x$ means $4 \times x$. Undo multiplying: divide both sides by $4$.
@step $x = 28 \div 4 = 7$.
@step Check: put $x = 7$ back in. $4 \times 7 = 28$. Both sides are equal.
@answer $x = 7$
:::

## Two operations

When there are two operations, undo them in reverse order. Undo adding or subtracting first. Then undo multiplying or dividing.

:::example Undo in reverse order
Solve $3x + 4 = 19$.
@step Undo adding $4$: subtract $4$ from both sides. $3x = 19 - 4 = 15$.
@step Undo multiplying by $3$: divide both sides by $3$. $x = 15 \div 3 = 5$.
@step Check: $3 \times 5 + 4 = 15 + 4 = 19$. Both sides equal $19$.
@answer $x = 5$
:::

There is more than one strategy. You can also **guess and check**. Try values for $x$ and see which one makes $3x + 4$ equal $19$.

:::

:::mistake Dividing first
In $3x + 4 = 19$, do not divide $19$ by $3$ first. The $4$ is added after multiplying, so take it away first.
:::

## Simplify each side first

If a side has like terms or numbers to work out, simplify it first. The simpler equation has the same solution.

:::example Simplify, then solve
Solve $2x + 3x + 4 = 20 + 9$.
@step Simplify the left side: $2x + 3x = 5x$. Now it is $5x + 4$.
@step Simplify the right side: $20 + 9 = 29$. The equation is now $5x + 4 = 29$.
@step Subtract $4$ from both sides: $5x = 25$. Divide both sides by $5$: $x = 5$.
@step Check in the first equation. Left: $2 \times 5 + 3 \times 5 + 4 = 10 + 15 + 4 = 29$. Right: $20 + 9 = 29$.
@answer $x = 5$
:::

## Writing equations for problems

1. Choose a letter for the unknown number.
2. Write an equation that tells the story.
3. Solve it and check your answer in the story.

:::example The class trip
A class trip costs \$50 for the bus plus \$4 for each student. The total cost is \$102. How many students are going?
@step Let $s$ be the number of students. The students cost $4s$ dollars, plus $50$ for the bus: $4s + 50 = 102$.
@step Subtract $50$ from both sides: $4s = 52$.
@step Divide both sides by $4$: $s = 13$.
@step Check: $4 \times 13 + 50 = 52 + 50 = 102$.
@answer $13$ students are going on the trip.
:::

:::example Two ages
Ana is $3$ years older than Ben. The sum of their ages is $25$. How old is Ben?
@step Let $b$ be Ben's age. Then Ana is $b + 3$. The equation is $b + (b + 3) = 25$.
@step Simplify the left side: $2b + 3 = 25$.
@step Subtract $3$: $2b = 22$. Divide by $2$: $b = 11$.
@step Check: Ana is $14$, and $11 + 14 = 25$.
@answer Ben is $11$ years old.
:::

:::check m6-equation-problems 3
:::

:::summary
- Solve an equation with inverse operations. Do the same thing to both sides.
- With two operations, undo adding or subtracting first, then multiplying or dividing.
- Simplify each side first. The simpler equation has the same solution.
- Check a solution by putting it back into the equation: both sides must be equal.
:::
