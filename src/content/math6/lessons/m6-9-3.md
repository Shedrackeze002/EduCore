The end wall of a barn is not a rectangle or a triangle. It is both! In this lesson you will find the area of shapes made of triangles and parallelograms.

## Splitting a shape into parts

:::definition Composite shape
A **composite shape** is made of two or more simpler shapes, such as triangles and parallelograms. Its area is the **sum** of the areas of its parts.
:::

A rectangle is a parallelogram with square corners, so its area is also base × height.

:::example The end of a barn
Find the area of the barn wall above.
@step Split the shape along the dashed line into a rectangle and a triangle.
@step Rectangle: $8 \times 5 = 40$
@step Triangle: base $8\ \text{m}$ and height $3\ \text{m}$, so $(8 \times 3) \div 2 = 12$.
@step Total: $40 + 12 = 52$
@answer The area of the wall is $52\ \text{m}^2$.
:::

## Many ways to split

You can split a shape in different ways. Every way gives the same total area. Here is one shape split two ways.

:::

:::example Split into a parallelogram and a triangle
@step The parallelogram has a base of $6\ \text{cm}$ and a height of $4\ \text{cm}$: $6 \times 4 = 24$.
@step The triangle has a base of $10 - 6 = 4\ \text{cm}$ and a height of $4\ \text{cm}$: $(4 \times 4) \div 2 = 8$.
@step Total: $24 + 8 = 32$
@answer The area is $32\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::

:::example Split into two triangles
@step The bottom triangle has a base of $10\ \text{cm}$ and a height of $4\ \text{cm}$: $(10 \times 4) \div 2 = 20$.
@step The top triangle has a base of $6\ \text{cm}$ (the top side) and a height of $4\ \text{cm}$: $(6 \times 4) \div 2 = 12$.
@step Total: $20 + 12 = 32$
@answer The area is $32\ \text{cm}^2$, the same as before.
:::

:::mistake Count every part once
Make sure the parts do not overlap and do not leave gaps. Every part of the shape must be counted exactly once.
:::

## Taking away a part

Sometimes it is easier to find the area of a big shape and **subtract** the part that is missing.

:::example A wall with an opening
A wall is a $10\ \text{m}$ by $6\ \text{m}$ rectangle with a triangular opening at the top. The opening has a base of $4\ \text{m}$ and a height of $3\ \text{m}$. Find the area of the wall.
@step Whole rectangle: $10 \times 6 = 60$
@step Triangle cut out: $(4 \times 3) \div 2 = 6$
@step $60 - 6 = 54$
@answer The area of the wall is $54\ \text{m}^2$.
:::

:::

:::summary
- A composite shape is made of simpler shapes, such as triangles and parallelograms.
- Split the shape, find the area of each part, and add.
- A shape can be split in many ways. Every way gives the same total.
- Sometimes it is easier to find a bigger area and subtract the missing part.
:::
