"Everything is $25\%$ off!" "$40\%$ of our class walks to school." Percents are everywhere. In this lesson, you will find a percent of a number using what you know about ratios.

## Percent means out of 100

:::definition Percentage
A **percentage** (or **percent**) compares a quantity to $100$. The symbol $\%$ means "out of $100$". $25\%$ means $25$ out of $100$, or $\dfrac{25}{100}$.
:::

The hundred grid has $100$ squares. $25$ of them are shaded, so $25\%$ is shaded. That is $\dfrac{25}{100} = \dfrac{1}{4}$ of the grid.

Some percents are friendly fractions:

| Percent | $10\%$ | $20\%$ | $25\%$ | $50\%$ | $75\%$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fraction | $\dfrac{1}{10}$ | $\dfrac{1}{5}$ | $\dfrac{1}{4}$ | $\dfrac{1}{2}$ | $\dfrac{3}{4}$ |

:::example Friendly percents
Find $25\%$ of $32$. Then find $75\%$ of $40$.
@step $25\% = \dfrac{1}{4}$. One quarter of $32$ is $32 \div 4 = 8$.
@step $75\% = \dfrac{3}{4}$. One quarter of $40$ is $40 \div 4 = 10$, so three quarters is $3 \times 10 = 30$.
@answer $25\%$ of $32$ is $8$, and $75\%$ of $40$ is $30$.
:::

## Percent of a number as a proportion

A percent is a ratio with $100$. To find a percent of a number, write a proportion.

:::example Thirty percent of sixty
Find $30\%$ of $60$.
@step $30\%$ means $30$ out of $100$. The answer, $x$, out of $60$ is the same ratio: $\dfrac{30}{100} = \dfrac{x}{60}$.
@step Simplify: $\dfrac{30}{100} = \dfrac{3}{10}$. Then $10 \times 6 = 60$, so $3 \times 6 = 18$.
@answer $30\%$ of $60$ is $18$.
:::

There is also a quick rule: **multiply the number by the percent, then divide by $100$.**

$$30 \times 60 = 1800 \qquad 1800 \div 100 = 18$$

:::tip The 10% trick
$10\%$ of a number is the number divided by $10$. $10\%$ of $60$ is $6$. Then $30\%$ is $3 \times 6 = 18$.
:::

:::mistake Dividing by the percent
$30\%$ of $60$ is **not** $60 \div 30 = 2$. A percent is "out of $100$". Multiply by $30$, then divide by $100$.
:::

## Percent problems

:::example A sale
A winter jacket costs \$60. It is on sale for $25\%$ off. What is the sale price?
@step The discount is $25\%$ of $60$: $60 \div 4 = 15$. That is \$15 off.
@step Subtract the discount: $60 - 15 = 45$.
@answer The sale price is \$45.
:::

:::example Walking to school
There are $30$ students in a class. $40\%$ of them walk to school. How many students do **not** walk?
@step $40\%$ of $30$: $40 \times 30 = 1200$, and $1200 \div 100 = 12$. So $12$ students walk.
@step The rest: $30 - 12 = 18$.
@answer $18$ students do not walk to school.
:::

:::tip Does it make sense?
A percent within $100\%$ is never more than the whole amount. $40\%$ is a bit less than half, and $12$ is a bit less than half of $30$.
:::

:::summary
- A percentage compares a quantity to $100$: $25\% = \dfrac{25}{100} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.
- To find a percent of a number, use a proportion, such as $\dfrac{30}{100} = \dfrac{x}{60}$.
- Or multiply the number by the percent and divide by $100$.
- In a sale, subtract the discount from the price.
:::
