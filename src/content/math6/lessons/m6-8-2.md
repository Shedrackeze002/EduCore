In a video game, a spaceship slides across the screen. On a garden plan, a flower bed is flipped to the other side of a path. In this lesson you will move shapes in the Cartesian plane and see how their coordinates change.

## Translations

:::definition Translation and image
A **translation** slides a shape without turning or flipping it. Every point moves the same distance in the same direction. The new shape is called the **image**. The image of point $A$ is written $A'$ (say "A prime").
:::

A translation has a **horizontal** part (right or left) and a **vertical** part (up or down). Triangle $ABC$ below is translated $5$ units right and $2$ units down.

:::example Translating a vertex
Find $B'$, the image of $B(-1,\ 1)$, after a translation $5$ units right and $2$ units down.
@step Right $5$ units changes $x$: $-1 + 5 = 4$.
@step Down $2$ units changes $y$: $1 - 2 = -1$.
@answer $B'(4,\ -1)$
:::

:::mistake Which coordinate changes?
Right and left change the $x$-coordinate. Up and down change the $y$-coordinate. Do not mix them up.
:::

:::

## Describing a translation

To describe a translation, compare one vertex with its image.

:::example From A to A'
In the picture above, $A$ is at $(-4,\ 1)$ and $A'$ is at $(1,\ -1)$. Describe the translation.
@step Across: from $x = -4$ to $x = 1$ is $5$ units to the right.
@step Up or down: from $y = 1$ to $y = -1$ is $2$ units down.
@answer The translation is $5$ units right and $2$ units down.
:::

## Reflections in the axes

:::definition Line of reflection
In a reflection, the **line of reflection** is the mirror line. Each point and its image are the same distance from the line, on opposite sides.
:::

Here, triangle $ABC$ is reflected in the **$y$-axis**. The $y$-coordinates stay the same. The $x$-coordinates change to their opposites.

:::example Reflecting in the y-axis
Find $B'$, the image of $B(4,\ 1)$, after a reflection in the $y$-axis.
@step $B$ is $4$ units to the right of the $y$-axis.
@step Its image is $4$ units to the left: $x = -4$. The $y$-coordinate stays $1$.
@answer $B'(-4,\ 1)$
:::

:::example Reflecting in the x-axis
Find $C'$, the image of $C(2,\ 4)$, after a reflection in the $x$-axis.
@step $C$ is $4$ units above the $x$-axis, so $C'$ is $4$ units below it: $y = -4$.
@step The $x$-coordinate stays $2$.
@answer $C'(2,\ -4)$
:::

:::tip Relating the coordinates
- Translation: add or subtract the same numbers for every vertex.
- Reflection in the $x$-axis: $(x,\ y) \to (x,\ -y)$.
- Reflection in the $y$-axis: $(x,\ y) \to (-x,\ y)$.
:::

:::summary
- A **translation** slides every point the same distance: right or left changes $x$, up or down changes $y$.
- Describe a translation by its horizontal part and its vertical part, such as "$5$ units right and $2$ units down".
- In a reflection in the $x$-axis, $x$ stays the same and $y$ changes to its opposite.
- In a reflection in the $y$-axis, $y$ stays the same and $x$ changes to its opposite.
:::
