At work, the same kind of calculation comes up again and again: the cost of some tickets, the pay for some hours, the fence around a yard. A short maths sentence with a letter in it can do the job every time. You just put in the numbers.

## Variables and expressions

:::definition Variable
A **variable** is a letter that stands for a number that can change, such as the number of tickets or the number of hours worked.
:::

:::definition Expression
An **expression** is a maths phrase made of numbers, variables and operations, such as $12n$ or $a + 3$. It has no equal sign.
:::

When a number is written right beside a letter, it means **multiply**. So $12n$ means $12 \times n$.

:::example Movie tickets
Movie tickets cost \$12 each. Write an expression for the cost, in dollars, of $n$ tickets.
@step Each ticket costs \$12. Two tickets cost $12 \times 2$. Three tickets cost $12 \times 3$.
@step For $n$ tickets, the cost is $12 \times n$.
@answer The expression is $12n$.
:::

Key words tell you which operation to use.

| Words in the problem | Operation | Example |
|---|---|---|
| each, per, every | multiply | \$16 per hour for $h$ hours: $16h$ |
| more than, plus, older, fee added | add | 3 years older than $a$: $a + 3$ |
| less, spent, drops, left | subtract | \$50 minus $s$ spent: $50 - s$ |
| shared equally, split | divide | \$60 shared by $p$ people: $\dfrac{60}{p}$ |

:::mistake Order in subtraction
Mila has \$50 and spends $s$ dollars. The money left is $50 - s$, not $s - 50$. Start with the amount you have, then take away.
:::

## Expressions with two parts

Many jobs pay or charge an amount **for each** hour or item, plus a **one-time** amount.

:::example Holiday pay
Jamal earns \$15 per hour, plus a \$20 bonus for working on a holiday. Write an expression for his pay for $h$ hours. Then find his pay for $6$ hours.
@step The hourly part changes with the hours: $15h$.
@step The bonus is paid once: $+\,20$.
@step Expression: $15h + 20$
@step For $6$ hours: $15 \times 6 + 20 = 90 + 20 = 110$
@answer The expression is $15h + 20$. Jamal earns \$110 for 6 hours.
:::

## Using a formula

:::definition Formula
A **formula** is a rule that uses variables to show how to calculate something, such as $P = 2l + 2w$ for the perimeter of a rectangle.
:::

:::definition Substitute
To **substitute** means to replace each variable with its number.
:::

:::example Fencing a garden
Use $P = 2l + 2w$ to find the perimeter of a garden $12\ \text{m}$ long and $7\ \text{m}$ wide.
@step Write the formula: $P = 2l + 2w$
@step Substitute $l = 12$ and $w = 7$: $P = 2(12) + 2(7)$
@step Multiply first: $P = 24 + 14$
@step Add: $P = 38$
@answer The perimeter is $38\ \text{m}$.
:::

:::example Pay for a shift
The formula $P = rh$ gives the pay $P$ for $h$ hours at a rate of $r$ dollars per hour. Find the pay for $7.5$ hours at \$16.50 per hour.
@step Substitute $r = 16.50$ and $h = 7.5$: $P = 16.50 \times 7.5$
@step $P = 123.75$
@answer The pay is \$123.75.
:::

:::mistake Adding before multiplying
A taxi fare is $C = 4 + 2k$. For $k = 10$, multiply first: $C = 4 + 20 = 24$. Do **not** add $4 + 2$ first; that gives $6 \times 10 = 60$, which is far too much.
:::

## Formulas in two-step jobs

Often a formula is only the first step. Use its answer for the next step.

:::example Change from a taxi ride
A taxi uses $C = 4.25 + 2.10k$, where $k$ is the distance in kilometres. Tia rides $12\ \text{km}$ and pays with \$40. How much change does she get?
@step Substitute $k = 12$: $C = 4.25 + 2.10 \times 12$
@step Multiply first: $C = 4.25 + 25.20 = 29.45$
@step Change: $40 - 29.45 = 10.55$
@answer Tia gets \$10.55 in change.
:::

:::check m104-use-formula 3
:::

:::summary
- A variable is a letter that stands for a number. An expression like $12n$ has no equal sign.
- A number beside a letter means multiply: $12n = 12 \times n$.
- "Each" or "per" means multiply. A one-time fee is added once: $15h + 20$.
- To use a formula, substitute the numbers, then multiply before you add.
- Use the answer from a formula for the next step of a job, such as finding change.
:::
