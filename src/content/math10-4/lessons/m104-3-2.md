A store sign says "25% off". Your safety quiz says 18 out of 20. A recipe uses $\frac{3}{4}$ of a cup of flour. These are all ways to show part of a whole. In this lesson you will change between fractions, decimals and percents so you can compare them.

## What is a percent?

:::definition Percent
**Percent** means "out of 100". The symbol is %. So $45\%$ means 45 out of 100, or $\frac{45}{100}$.
:::

The whole is always $100\%$. In the circle graph below, $45\% + 35\% + 20\% = 100\%$.

:::example Out of 100
At a coffee shop, 20 of 100 customers paid with cash. What percent is that?
@step $20$ out of $100$ is $\frac{20}{100}$.
@answer $20\%$ of the customers paid with cash.
:::

## Decimals and percents

To change a **decimal to a percent**, multiply by $100$. The decimal point moves 2 places to the right.

To change a **percent to a decimal**, divide by $100$. The decimal point moves 2 places to the left.

:::example Decimal to percent
Write $0.35$ as a percent.
@step Multiply by $100$: $0.35 \times 100 = 35$
@answer $0.35 = 35\%$
:::

:::example Percent to decimal
Write $6\%$ as a decimal.
@step Divide by $100$. Think of $6$ as $6.0$ and move the decimal point 2 places left. Put a zero in the empty place.
@step $6 \div 100 = 0.06$
@answer $6\% = 0.06$
:::

:::mistake 6% is not 0.6
$0.6$ is $60\%$. When a percent has only one digit, put a zero right after the decimal point: $6\% = 0.06$ and $8\% = 0.08$.
:::

## Fractions, decimals and percents

There are two ways to change a fraction to a percent.

1. If the bottom number goes evenly into $100$, make an equal fraction out of $100$.
2. If not, divide the top by the bottom to get a decimal. Then multiply by $100$.

:::example An equal fraction out of 100
Write $\frac{3}{4}$ as a percent.
@step $4 \times 25 = 100$, so multiply the top and the bottom by $25$: $\frac{3 \times 25}{4 \times 25} = \frac{75}{100}$
@answer $\frac{3}{4} = 75\%$
:::

:::example Divide first
Write $\frac{3}{8}$ as a decimal and as a percent.
@step Divide: $3 \div 8 = 0.375$
@step Multiply by $100$: $0.375 \times 100 = 37.5$
@answer $\frac{3}{8} = 0.375 = 37.5\%$
:::

:::definition Lowest terms
A fraction is in **lowest terms** when no whole number bigger than 1 divides evenly into both the top and the bottom.
:::

:::example A percent as a fraction
Write $35\%$ as a fraction in lowest terms.
@step $35\% = \frac{35}{100}$
@step Both numbers divide by $5$: $\frac{35 \div 5}{100 \div 5} = \frac{7}{20}$
@answer $35\% = \frac{7}{20}$
:::

:::mistake Writing the digits as a percent
$\frac{3}{4}$ is **not** $34\%$. The digits of a fraction are not the percent. Change the fraction to "out of 100" first.
:::

## Percents in real life

:::example A safety quiz
Amara got 21 out of 25 questions right on a workplace safety quiz. What is her score as a percent? What percent did she get wrong?
@step Write a fraction and divide: $21 \div 25 = 0.84$
@step Multiply by $100$: $0.84 \times 100 = 84$, so she scored $84\%$.
@step The whole quiz is $100\%$: $100\% - 84\% = 16\%$
@answer Amara scored $84\%$. She got $16\%$ wrong.
:::

:::tip Percents make comparing easy
Is 17 out of 20 better than $82\%$? Change 17 out of 20 to a percent: $17 \div 20 = 0.85 = 85\%$. Since $85\% > 82\%$, 17 out of 20 is the better score.
:::

:::

:::summary
- Percent means "out of 100". The whole is $100\%$.
- Decimal to percent: multiply by $100$. Percent to decimal: divide by $100$.
- Fraction to percent: make an equal fraction out of $100$, or divide the top by the bottom and multiply by $100$.
- Percent to fraction: write it over $100$, then simplify to lowest terms.
- Know the common ones: $\frac{1}{2} = 50\%$, $\frac{1}{4} = 25\%$, $\frac{3}{4} = 75\%$, $\frac{1}{10} = 10\%$.
:::
