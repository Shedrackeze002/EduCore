Your taxi fare was \$22. How many kilometres did you ride? You worked some hours and earned \$252. How many hours was that? Questions like these work **backwards** from an answer. An equation helps you do that step by step.

## What is an equation?

:::definition Equation
An **equation** is a maths sentence with an equal sign. Both sides have the same value, like a balanced scale. For example, $x + 7 = 15$.
:::

:::definition Solve
To **solve** an equation means to find the value of the variable that makes it true.
:::

For $x + 7 = 15$, ask: "What number plus $7$ makes $15$?" The answer is $8$, because $8 + 7 = 15$. For bigger numbers, guessing is slow. There is a better way.

## Inverse operations

:::definition Inverse operation
An **inverse operation** undoes another operation. Adding and subtracting are inverses. Multiplying and dividing are inverses.
:::

To get the variable by itself, do the inverse operation to **both sides**. The scale stays balanced.

:::example Undo adding
Solve $x + 7 = 15$.
@step $7$ is added to $x$. The inverse is to subtract $7$.
@step Subtract $7$ from both sides: $x = 15 - 7$
@step $x = 8$
@step Check: $8 + 7 = 15$. It works.
@answer $x = 8$
:::

:::example Undo multiplying
Solve $4x = 36$.
@step $4x$ means $4 \times x$. The inverse is to divide by $4$.
@step Divide both sides by $4$: $x = 36 \div 4 = 9$
@step Check: $4 \times 9 = 36$. It works.
@answer $x = 9$
:::

The same idea works for the others. For $x - 4 = 9$, add $4$: $x = 13$. For $x \div 3 = 5$, multiply by $3$: $x = 15$.

:::mistake Doing the same operation
For $x + 7 = 15$, some students add $7$ and get $x = 22$. Check it: $22 + 7 = 29$, not $15$. Always do the **opposite** operation, and always check.
:::

## Equations from real life

:::example Hours worked
Kai earns \$18 per hour at a hardware store. Last week he earned \$252. How many hours did he work?
@step Let $h$ be the number of hours. Pay is the rate times the hours: $18h = 252$
@step Divide both sides by $18$: $h = 252 \div 18 = 14$
@step Check: $18 \times 14 = 252$. It works.
@answer Kai worked $14$ hours.
:::

:::

## Two-step equations

In $3x + 5 = 26$, two things happen to $x$: it is multiplied by $3$, then $5$ is added. To solve it, undo them in the **reverse** order: undo the adding first, then the multiplying.

:::example Solve a two-step equation
Solve $3x + 5 = 26$.
@step Undo the adding: subtract $5$ from both sides. $3x = 26 - 5 = 21$
@step Undo the multiplying: divide both sides by $3$. $x = 21 \div 3 = 7$
@step Check: $3 \times 7 + 5 = 21 + 5 = 26$. It works.
@answer $x = 7$
:::

:::check m104-solve-two-step 1
:::

:::example A taxi ride
A taxi charges \$4 to start plus \$2 for each kilometre. Lina's fare was \$22. How far did she ride?
@step Let $k$ be the kilometres. The fare is $2k + 4$, so $2k + 4 = 22$.
@step Subtract $4$ from both sides: $2k = 18$
@step Divide both sides by $2$: $k = 9$
@step Check: $2 \times 9 + 4 = 18 + 4 = 22$. It works.
@answer Lina rode $9\ \text{km}$.
:::

:::mistake Dividing first
For $3x + 5 = 26$, do not divide by $3$ first. The $5$ is not multiplied by $3$, so subtract it before you divide.
:::

:::tip Does the answer make sense?
A phone plan is \$25 a month plus \$10 per GB. The bill is \$65, so $10g + 25 = 65$ and $g = 4$. Four GB at \$10 is \$40, plus \$25 is \$65. A quick check like this catches most mistakes.
:::

:::

:::check m104-solve-two-step 3
:::

:::summary
- An equation has an equal sign. Both sides balance.
- Solve by doing the inverse operation to both sides: subtract to undo adding, divide to undo multiplying.
- For two steps, undo the adding or subtracting first, then the multiplying or dividing.
- Always check: put your answer back into the equation.
- In word problems, let a letter stand for the unknown, write the equation, then solve.
:::
