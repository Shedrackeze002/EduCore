As a point travels around the unit circle, its height rises and falls in the same pattern on every lap. Graph that height against the angle and you get the wave behind sound, light, tides and alternating current: the sine curve.

:::definition Periodic function, period and amplitude
A **periodic function** repeats its values at regular intervals. The **period** is the length of the shortest interval that repeats. The **amplitude** of a sinusoidal curve is half the distance between its maximum and minimum values. A **sinusoidal curve** is any graph with the shape of $y = \sin x$.
:::

| | $y = \sin x$ | $y = \cos x$ |
|---|---|---|
| Domain | $\{x \mid x \in \mathbb{R}\}$ | $\{x \mid x \in \mathbb{R}\}$ |
| Range | $\{y \mid -1 \le y \le 1,\ y \in \mathbb{R}\}$ | $\{y \mid -1 \le y \le 1,\ y \in \mathbb{R}\}$ |
| Period, amplitude | $2\pi$, $1$ | $2\pi$, $1$ |
| Zeros | $x = n\pi$ | $x = \frac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$ |
| Maximums | $x = \frac{\pi}{2} + 2\pi n$ | $x = 2\pi n$ |

The cosine graph is the sine graph moved $\frac{\pi}{2}$ to the left.

## Period: the effect of b

In $y = \sin bx$, the angle $bx$ completes one cycle, from $0$ to $2\pi$, while $x$ goes from $0$ to $\dfrac{2\pi}{b}$. So the graph is stretched horizontally by a factor of $\dfrac{1}{|b|}$.

:::definition Period of y = a sin bx and y = a cos bx
$$\text{period} = \frac{2\pi}{|b|} \text{ radians} \quad \text{or} \quad \frac{360^\circ}{|b|}$$
:::

$y = \sin 2x$ (purple) completes two cycles while $y = \sin x$ (grey) completes one: its period is $\pi$.

:::example Amplitude and period
Determine the amplitude and the period of $y = 4\cos\dfrac{1}{3}x$.
@step The amplitude is $|a| = 4$.
@step Period $= \dfrac{2\pi}{1/3} = 6\pi$, which is $\dfrac{360^\circ}{1/3} = 1080^\circ$.
@answer Amplitude $4$, period $6\pi$ (or $1080^\circ$)
:::

:::example From the period to b
$y = \sin bx$ with $b > 0$ has a period of $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$. Determine $b$.
@step Rearrange $\text{period} = \dfrac{2\pi}{b}$ to $b = \dfrac{2\pi}{\text{period}}$.
@step $b = \dfrac{2\pi}{2\pi/3} = 3$
@answer $b = 3$
:::

:::mistake Multiplying instead of dividing
A larger $b$ squeezes the graph: $y = \sin 3x$ has a **shorter** period, $\frac{2\pi}{3}$, not $6\pi$.
:::

:::summary
- $y = \sin x$ and $y = \cos x$ have domain all real numbers, range $-1 \le y \le 1$, period $2\pi$ and amplitude $1$.
- Zeros: $\sin x = 0$ at $x = n\pi$; $\cos x = 0$ at $x = \frac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$.
- In $y = a\sin bx$ or $y = a\cos bx$, the amplitude is $|a|$ and the period is $\frac{2\pi}{|b|}$ (or $\frac{360^\circ}{|b|}$).
- $a < 0$ reflects the graph in the $x$-axis.
:::
