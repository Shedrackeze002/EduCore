The Grade 8 classes hoped to raise \$2,400 and raised $135\%$ of their goal. A bank pays $0.75\%$ interest on savings. To use percents like these, you need to find a percent of a number, even when the percent is bigger than 100% or smaller than 1%.

## Finding a percent of a number

To find a percent of a number, change the percent to a decimal, then multiply.

:::example A percent you know
Find $35\%$ of $60$.
@step $35\% = 0.35$
@step $0.35 \times 60 = 21$
@answer $35\%$ of $60$ is $21$.
:::

:::tip Estimate first
$50\%$ is half and $100\%$ is the whole number. Use these to check that your answer is about the right size.
:::

## Percents greater than 100%

$100\%$ of a number is the number itself. So **more than $100\%$** of a number is **more than the number**.

:::example 150% of a number
Find $150\%$ of $80$.
@step $150\% = 1.5$, so multiply: $1.5 \times 80 = 120$
@step Check with mental math: $150\% = 100\% + 50\%$. $100\%$ of $80$ is $80$ and $50\%$ of $80$ is $40$. $80 + 40 = 120$.
@answer $150\%$ of $80$ is $120$.
:::

:::mistake Too small to be right
If you find $150\%$ of $80$ and get a number less than $80$, something went wrong. A common slip is to use $0.15$ instead of $1.5$.
:::

:::

## Percents less than 1%

**Less than $1\%$** of a number is **less than one hundredth** of it. Find $1\%$ first, then take part of it.

:::example A quarter of a percent
Find $0.25\%$ of $2000$.
@step $1\%$ of $2000$ is $2000 \div 100 = 20$.
@step $0.25\%$ is one quarter of $1\%$: $20 \div 4 = 5$.
@step Check with the decimal method: $0.25\% = 0.0025$ and $0.0025 \times 2000 = 5$.
@answer $0.25\%$ of $2000$ is $5$.
:::

:::example A fraction of a percent
Find $\frac{1}{2}\%$ of $600$.
@step $\frac{1}{2}\% = 0.5\% = 0.005$
@step $0.005 \times 600 = 3$
@answer $\frac{1}{2}\%$ of $600$ is $3$.
:::

:::mistake 0.25% is not 25%
$25\%$ of $2000$ is $500$. But $0.25\%$ of $2000$ is only $5$. Divide the percent by $100$ carefully: $0.25\% = 0.0025$.
:::

:::

## Solving problems

:::example Interest on savings
Hana puts \$1,200 in a savings account that pays $0.75\%$ interest per year. How much interest does she earn in one year?
@step $0.75\% = 0.0075$
@step $0.0075 \times 1200 = 9$
@answer Hana earns \$9.00 in interest.
:::

:::example Beating a goal
The Grade 8 classes set a goal of \$2,400 for a class trip. They raised $135\%$ of their goal. How much did they raise? How much over the goal is that?
@step $135\% = 1.35$, and $1.35 \times 2400 = 3240$
@step Over the goal: $3240 - 2400 = 840$
@answer They raised \$3,240.00, which is \$840.00 over their goal.
:::

:::check m8-percent-of 3
:::

:::summary
- To find a percent of a number, write the percent as a decimal and multiply.
- More than $100\%$ of a number is more than the number: $150\%$ of $80$ is $120$.
- Less than $1\%$ of a number is less than one hundredth of it: find $1\%$ first, then take part of it.
- Estimate first, so you can tell whether your answer is the right size.
:::
