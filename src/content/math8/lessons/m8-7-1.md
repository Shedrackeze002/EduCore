A diver goes down at a steady rate and is $15$ m below the surface after $3$ minutes. How far does the diver go down each minute? Questions like this lead to an **equation**. In this lesson you will solve equations that need just one step, including equations with negative numbers.

## Equations as a balance

:::definition Equation
An **equation** is a maths sentence with an equal sign. Both sides have the same value, like the two sides of a balanced scale. For example, $3x = 12$.
:::

:::definition Solve
To **solve** an equation is to find the value of the variable that makes the equation true. That value is the **solution**.
:::

:::definition Algebra tiles
**Algebra tiles** are models for algebra. A long tile stands for the variable $x$, and a small square stands for $1$.
:::

The balance below shows $3x = 12$: three $x$-tiles on the left balance twelve unit tiles on the right.

## Solving ax = b

:::definition Inverse operation
An **inverse operation** undoes another operation. Multiplying and dividing are inverses, and adding and subtracting are inverses.
:::

In $3x = 12$, $x$ is multiplied by $3$. To get $x$ by itself, divide **both sides** by $3$. With the tiles, you share the $12$ unit tiles equally among the $3$ $x$-tiles: each $x$-tile matches $4$ unit tiles.

:::example Solve 3x = 12
Solve $3x = 12$.
@step $x$ is multiplied by $3$. The inverse is dividing by $3$.
@step Divide both sides by $3$: $\dfrac{3x}{3} = \dfrac{12}{3}$, so $x = 4$.
@step Check by substitution: $3(4) = 12$. It works.
@answer $x = 4$
:::

:::check m8-equation-one-step 1
:::

The same steps work with negative integers. Use the sign rules for dividing: same signs give a positive quotient, different signs give a negative quotient.

:::example Solve −4x = 28
Solve $-4x = 28$.
@step $x$ is multiplied by $-4$. Divide both sides by $-4$.
@step $x = 28 \div (-4) = -7$. The signs are different, so the quotient is negative.
@step Check: $-4(-7) = 28$. It works.
@answer $x = -7$
:::

:::mistake Dropping the sign
For $-4x = 28$, some students divide by $4$ and get $x = 7$. Check it: $-4(7) = -28$, not $28$. Divide by the whole coefficient, $-4$, sign and all.
:::

## Solving x/a = b

In $\dfrac{x}{a} = b$, $x$ is divided by $a$. The inverse of dividing is multiplying, so multiply both sides by $a$.

:::example Solve x/(−3) = 5
Solve $\dfrac{x}{-3} = 5$.
@step $x$ is divided by $-3$. Multiply both sides by $-3$.
@step $x = 5 \times (-3) = -15$. The signs are different, so the product is negative.
@step Check: $\dfrac{-15}{-3} = 5$. It works.
@answer $x = -15$
:::

:::tip Always check
Substituting your answer back into the equation takes a few seconds and catches most sign mistakes.
:::

## One-step equations in real life

:::example The diver
A diver goes down at a steady rate. After $3$ minutes, the diver’s elevation has changed by $-15$ m. Let $r$ be the change in elevation each minute. Solve $3r = -15$.
@step $r$ is multiplied by $3$. Divide both sides by $3$.
@step $r = -15 \div 3 = -5$
@step Check: $3(-5) = -15$. It works.
@answer The diver’s elevation changes by $-5$ m each minute. The diver goes down $5$ m every minute.
:::

:::example Sharing a bill
Four friends share the cost of a pizza order equally, and each pays \$9. Let $p$ be the total cost, in dollars. Solve $\dfrac{p}{4} = 9$.
@step $p$ is divided by $4$. Multiply both sides by $4$.
@step $p = 9 \times 4 = 36$
@step Check: $36 \div 4 = 9$. It works.
@answer The pizza order cost \$36.
:::

:::check m8-equation-one-step 3
:::

:::summary
- An equation is like a balance: whatever you do to one side, do to the other.
- Solve $ax = b$ by dividing both sides by $a$.
- Solve $\dfrac{x}{a} = b$ by multiplying both sides by $a$.
- Use the integer sign rules, and check your solution by substituting it into the equation.
:::
