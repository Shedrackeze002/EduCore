Kiona’s hockey team won $9$ games and lost $6$. A trail mix has $3$ cups of peanuts for every $2$ cups of raisins. A **ratio** is a way to compare amounts like these.

## What is a ratio?

:::definition Ratio
A **ratio** compares two or more quantities measured in the same units. The numbers in a ratio are its **terms**. The ratio of $5$ to $3$ can be written $5 : 3$, "$5$ to $3$" or $\dfrac{5}{3}$.
:::

Order matters. The quantity named first in the question goes first in the ratio.

:::example Marbles in a bag
A bag has $5$ red marbles and $3$ blue marbles. Write the ratio of red marbles to blue marbles, and the ratio of blue marbles to red marbles.
@step Red to blue: red goes first, so the ratio is $5 : 3$.
@step Blue to red: blue goes first, so the ratio is $3 : 5$.
@answer Red to blue is $5 : 3$. Blue to red is $3 : 5$.
:::

:::mistake Switching the order
"The ratio of blue to red" is $3 : 5$, not $5 : 3$. Write the terms in the same order as the words.
:::

## Part-to-part and part-to-whole ratios

:::definition Part-to-part ratio
A **part-to-part ratio** compares one part of a group with another part of the same group.
:::

:::definition Part-to-whole ratio
A **part-to-whole ratio** compares one part with the whole group. It can also be written as a fraction or a percent.
:::

:::example A Grade 8 class
A class has $12$ girls and $13$ boys. Write the ratio of girls to boys and the ratio of girls to all the students. What percent of the class are girls?
@step Part to part: girls to boys is $12 : 13$.
@step The whole class is $12 + 13 = 25$ students. Part to whole: girls to all students is $12 : 25$.
@step As a fraction, $\dfrac{12}{25}$. As a percent, $\dfrac{12}{25} = \dfrac{48}{100} = 48\%$.
@answer Girls to boys is $12 : 13$. Girls to all students is $12 : 25$, so $48\%$ of the class are girls.
:::

## Equivalent ratios

:::definition Equivalent ratios
**Equivalent ratios** make the same comparison. To get an equivalent ratio, multiply or divide **every** term by the same number (not zero).
:::

A juice recipe uses $1$ cup of concentrate for every $3$ cups of water. The table shows equivalent ratios. Each row is the first row multiplied by $2$, $3$ or $4$.

:::

:::example Find the missing term
Find the missing term: $3 : 5 = ? : 20$.
@step The second term went from $5$ to $20$, and $5 \times 4 = 20$.
@step Multiply the first term by $4$ too: $3 \times 4 = 12$.
@answer $3 : 5 = 12 : 20$, so the missing term is $12$.
:::

:::definition Lowest terms
A ratio is in **lowest terms** when its terms have no common factor other than $1$. To write a ratio in lowest terms, divide every term by the greatest common factor (GCF).
:::

:::example Writing a ratio in lowest terms
Write $12 : 18$ in lowest terms.
@step The GCF of $12$ and $18$ is $6$.
@step $12 \div 6 = 2$ and $18 \div 6 = 3$.
@answer $12 : 18 = 2 : 3$
:::

:::mistake Adding the same number
$2 : 3$ is **not** equivalent to $4 : 5$. Adding $2$ to each term changes the comparison. Equivalent ratios come from multiplying or dividing, never from adding or subtracting.
:::

## Three-term ratios

:::definition Three-term ratio
A **three-term ratio** compares three quantities, like $3 : 2 : 1$. Multiply or divide **all three** terms by the same number to get an equivalent ratio.
:::

:::example Trail mix
Kai’s trail mix has $6$ cups of peanuts, $4$ cups of raisins and $2$ cups of chocolate chips. Write the ratio of peanuts to raisins to chocolate chips in lowest terms. What fraction of the mix is peanuts?
@step The GCF of $6$, $4$ and $2$ is $2$. Divide each term by $2$: $6 : 4 : 2 = 3 : 2 : 1$.
@step The whole mix is $6 + 4 + 2 = 12$ cups. Peanuts to the whole mix: $\dfrac{6}{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
@answer The ratio is $3 : 2 : 1$, and $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the mix is peanuts.
:::

:::example A bead kit
The ratio of red to blue to green beads in a kit is $2 : 3 : 5$. There are $12$ red beads. How many green beads are there? How many beads are there in all?
@step $2 \times 6 = 12$, so multiply every term by $6$: $2 : 3 : 5 = 12 : 18 : 30$.
@step There are $30$ green beads. In all: $12 + 18 + 30 = 60$.
@answer There are $30$ green beads and $60$ beads in all.
:::

:::tip Check with the total
The ratio $2 : 3 : 5$ has $2 + 3 + 5 = 10$ parts. The total, $60$, is $10 \times 6$. The scale factor works for the total too.
:::

:::

:::summary
- A ratio compares quantities in a given order: $a : b$, "$a$ to $b$" or $\dfrac{a}{b}$.
- A part-to-part ratio compares two parts. A part-to-whole ratio compares a part with the total, and can be written as a fraction or a percent.
- Equivalent ratios: multiply or divide every term by the same number.
- Lowest terms: divide every term by the greatest common factor.
- Three-term ratios work the same way as two-term ratios.
:::
