"$35\%$ of students walk to school." "Your phone battery is at $0.8$." "$\frac{3}{4}$ of the class plays a sport." These are three ways to show part of a whole. In this lesson you will change between percents, fractions and decimals, and find a percent of a number.

## What a percent is

:::definition Percent
**Percent** means "out of $100$". The symbol is $\%$. So $35\%$ means $35$ out of $100$, or $\frac{35}{100}$. The whole is $100\%$.
:::

In this hundred grid, $35$ of the $100$ squares are shaded. That is $35\%$ of the grid.

:::example Three ways
Write the shaded part of the grid as a percent, a fraction and a decimal.
@step $35$ out of $100$ squares are shaded: $35\%$.
@step As a fraction: $\frac{35}{100}$. Divide both by $5$: $\frac{7}{20}$.
@step As a decimal: $35$ hundredths is $0.35$.
@answer $35\% = \frac{7}{20} = 0.35$
:::

## Percents and decimals

To change a **percent to a decimal**, divide by $100$: move the decimal point $2$ places to the left.

To change a **decimal to a percent**, multiply by $100$: move the decimal point $2$ places to the right.

:::example Four conversions
Write $45\%$ and $7\%$ as decimals. Write $0.6$ and $0.125$ as percents.
@step $45 \div 100 = 0.45$ and $7 \div 100 = 0.07$
@step $0.6 \times 100 = 60$, so $0.6 = 60\%$
@step $0.125 \times 100 = 12.5$, so $0.125 = 12.5\%$
@answer $45\% = 0.45$, $7\% = 0.07$, $0.6 = 60\%$, $0.125 = 12.5\%$
:::

:::mistake 7% is not 0.7
$0.7$ is $70\%$. A one-digit percent needs a zero after the decimal point: $7\% = 0.07$.
:::

## Percents and fractions

To change a **percent to a fraction**, write it over $100$ and simplify. To change a **fraction to a percent**, make an equivalent fraction out of $100$, or divide and then multiply by $100$.

:::example Percent to fraction
Write $36\%$ as a fraction in lowest terms.
@step $36\% = \frac{36}{100}$
@step Divide both by $4$: $\frac{9}{25}$
@answer $36\% = \frac{9}{25}$
:::

:::example Fraction to percent
Write $\frac{3}{8}$ as a percent.
@step $100$ is not a multiple of $8$, so divide: $3 \div 8 = 0.375$
@step Multiply by $100$: $0.375 \times 100 = 37.5$
@answer $\frac{3}{8} = 37.5\%$
:::

## Percent of a number

To find a percent of a number, you can:

- use a benchmark fraction ($25\% = \frac{1}{4}$, $50\% = \frac{1}{2}$),
- build it from $10\%$ (divide by $10$) and $5\%$ (half of $10\%$), or
- change the percent to a decimal and multiply.

:::example A benchmark
What is $25\%$ of $36$?
@step $25\%$ is one quarter, so divide by $4$.
@answer $36 \div 4 = 9$
:::

:::example Using 10% and 5%
Find $15\%$ of $64$ with mental math.
@step $10\%$ of $64$ is $64 \div 10 = 6.4$.
@step $5\%$ is half of that: $3.2$.
@answer $15\%$ of $64$ is $6.4 + 3.2 = 9.6$.
:::

:::example With a calculator
$35\%$ of the $240$ students at a school take the bus. How many students take the bus?
@step Estimate: $35\%$ is a little more than $\frac{1}{3}$, and $\frac{1}{3}$ of $240$ is $80$. Expect a little more than $80$.
@step Change the percent to a decimal: $35\% = 0.35$
@step Multiply: $0.35 \times 240 = 84$
@answer $84$ students take the bus.
:::

:::summary
- Percent means "out of $100$".
- Percent to decimal: divide by $100$. Decimal to percent: multiply by $100$.
- Percent to fraction: write it over $100$ and simplify. Fraction to percent: make it out of $100$, or divide and multiply by $100$.
- Percent of a number: use a benchmark, build it from $10\%$ and $5\%$, or multiply by the decimal.
:::
