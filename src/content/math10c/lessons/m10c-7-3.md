The third standard way to write a linear relation is **general form**. It has no fractions, and every line — even a vertical one — can be written this way.

## General form

:::definition General form
$$Ax + By + C = 0$$
where $A$ is a whole number (positive, or 0), and $B$ and $C$ are integers.
:::

Examples: $2x - 3y + 6 = 0$, $\ x + 4 = 0$ (vertical), $\ y - 5 = 0$ (horizontal).

## Converting to general form

1. Clear fractions by multiplying every term by the lowest common denominator.
2. Move all terms to the left side so the right side is 0.
3. If the $x$-coefficient is negative, multiply every term by $-1$.

:::example From slope-intercept form
Write $y = \tfrac23 x - 4$ in general form.
@step Multiply by 3: $3y = 2x - 12$
@step Move everything left: $-2x + 3y + 12 = 0$
@step Make $A$ positive: $2x - 3y - 12 = 0$
:::

:::example From slope-point form
Write $y + 1 = -\tfrac12(x - 6)$ in general form.
@step Multiply by 2: $2y + 2 = -(x - 6) = -x + 6$
@answer $x + 2y - 4 = 0$
:::

## Converting to slope-intercept form

Isolate $y$.

:::example From general form
Write $4x - 2y + 6 = 0$ in slope-intercept form.
@step $-2y = -4x - 6$
@answer $y = 2x + 3$
:::

## Features from general form

For $Ax + By + C = 0$ with $B \ne 0$:

- slope $m = -\dfrac{A}{B}$
- $x$-intercept: set $y = 0$, so $x = -\dfrac{C}{A}$
- $y$-intercept: set $x = 0$, so $y = -\dfrac{C}{B}$

General form is especially quick for **graphing with intercepts**.

:::example Intercepts and slope
For $3x + 5y - 15 = 0$, find the slope and intercepts.
@step $m = -\dfrac35$
@step $y = 0$: $3x = 15$, $x$-intercept $5$
@step $x = 0$: $5y = 15$, $y$-intercept $3$
:::

## Equivalent forms and graphs

The same line can be written in many ways. To decide if two equations are equivalent, rewrite both in slope-intercept form (or general form with no common factor) and compare.

:::example Equivalent?
Are $y - 1 = 2(x + 3)$ and $2x - y + 7 = 0$ equivalent?
@step First: $y = 2x + 7$
@step Second: $y = 2x + 7$
@answer Yes — they describe the same line.
:::

| Form | Best for |
|---|---|
| $y = mx + b$ | reading slope and $y$-intercept; graphing from $(0, b)$ |
| $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$ | writing an equation from a point and slope |
| $Ax + By + C = 0$ | graphing with intercepts; no fractions; vertical lines |

:::summary
- General form: $Ax + By + C = 0$, $A \ge 0$, all integers.
- To convert: clear fractions, move terms left, make $A$ positive.
- From general form: $m = -\tfrac{A}{B}$; intercepts by setting the other variable to 0.
- Compare forms by rewriting them the same way.
:::
