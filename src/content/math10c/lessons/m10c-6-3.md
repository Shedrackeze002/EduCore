Road signs warn drivers about steep hills with a percent grade. Roofers talk about the “pitch” of a roof. Both describe **slope** — how steep a line is.

## Rise over run

:::definition Slope
The **slope** $m$ of a line segment or line is
$$m = \frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} = \frac{\text{vertical change}}{\text{horizontal change}}$$
Moving from left to right, rise is positive going up and negative going down.
:::

## The slope formula

For two points $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$:

$$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$$

:::example Using the formula
Find the slope of the line through $(-2, 5)$ and $(4, -7)$.
@step $m = \dfrac{-7 - 5}{4 - (-2)} = \dfrac{-12}{6}$
@answer $m = -2$
:::

:::mistake Mixing the order
Subtract in the same order on the top and the bottom. $\dfrac{-7 - 5}{-2 - 4}$ mixes the order and gives the wrong sign.
:::

Why does any pair of points give the same slope? The right triangles formed by different pairs of points on a line are **similar**, so the ratio rise : run is always the same.

## Four kinds of slope

| Line | Slope |
|---|---|
| rises left to right | positive |
| falls left to right | negative |
| horizontal | $0$ (rise is 0) |
| vertical | **undefined** (run is 0, and you cannot divide by 0) |

## Slope as a rate of change

In a context, slope has units: “dependent units per independent unit”.

:::example A burning candle
A candle is $24\ \text{cm}$ tall after 2 h and $15\ \text{cm}$ tall after 5 h. Find and interpret the slope.
@step $m = \dfrac{15 - 24}{5 - 2} = \dfrac{-9}{3} = -3$
@answer $-3\ \text{cm/h}$: the candle gets 3 cm shorter each hour.
:::

## Using slope to find points and draw lines

A slope of $\dfrac{2}{3}$ means “up 2 for every 3 to the right” (or down 2 for every 3 to the left).

:::example Drawing a line
Draw the line through $(-1, 2)$ with slope $-\dfrac{3}{2}$.
@step Plot $(-1, 2)$.
@step From there go right 2 and down 3 to $(1, -1)$.
@step Draw the line through both points.
:::

:::example A missing coordinate
The line through $(2, 3)$ and $(6, k)$ has slope $\tfrac34$. Find $k$.
@step $\dfrac{k - 3}{6 - 2} = \dfrac34$
@step $k - 3 = 3$
@answer $k = 6$
:::

:::summary
- $m = \dfrac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$; any two points on a line give the same slope.
- Positive rises, negative falls, horizontal is 0, vertical is undefined.
- In context, slope is a rate of change with units.
- From a point, use rise and run to draw the line or find more points.
:::
