A phone plan costs \$20 a month plus \$5 per gigabyte. Every extra gigabyte adds exactly the same amount. Relationships like this, with a **constant rate of change**, are called **linear relations** — their graphs are straight lines.

## What makes a relation linear?

:::definition Linear relation
A relation with a **constant rate of change**. Its graph is a straight line.
:::

:::definition Rate of change
$$\text{rate of change} = \frac{\text{change in dependent variable}}{\text{change in independent variable}} = \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}$$
It is usually written with units, such as \$5 per gigabyte or $-8\ \text{L/min}$.
:::

You can recognize a linear relation in every representation:

| Representation | Linear when… |
|---|---|
| Situation | the quantity changes by the same amount for each equal step |
| Table | $\Delta y \div \Delta x$ is the same between every pair of rows |
| Graph | the points lie on a straight line |
| Equation | it can be written as $Ax + By + C = 0$ (no $x^2$, $xy$, $\tfrac1x$, $\sqrt{x}$, …) |

## Tables of values

:::example Checking a table
Is this relation linear?

| $x$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | 5 | 8 | 11 | 14 |

@step $x$ increases by 1 each time; $y$ increases by 3 each time.
@answer The rate of change is constant ($3$), so it is linear.
:::

:::example Unequal steps
Is this relation linear?

| $x$ | 1 | 2 | 4 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | 3 | 5 | 9 | 15 |

@step Rates: $\tfrac{2}{1} = 2$, $\tfrac{4}{2} = 2$, $\tfrac{6}{3} = 2$
@answer The rate is always 2, so it is linear — even though the $x$-steps are not equal.
:::

:::mistake Only looking at the y-differences
When the $x$-values do not go up by equal amounts, compare $\dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}$, not just $\Delta y$.
:::

## Equations

An equation is linear if each variable appears only to the first power, variables are not multiplied together, and no variable is in a denominator, a radical or an exponent.

- Linear: $y = 3x - 2$, $\ 2x + 5y = 10$, $\ y = 4$, $\ x = -1$
- Not linear: $y = x^2$, $\ xy = 6$, $\ y = \tfrac{1}{x}$, $\ y = \sqrt{x}$, $\ y = 2^x$

## Rate of change in context

:::example Draining a tank
A tank holds $600\ \text{L}$ after 2 min and $440\ \text{L}$ after 10 min, draining at a constant rate. Find the rate of change.
@step $\dfrac{440 - 600}{10 - 2} = \dfrac{-160}{8} = -20$
@answer $-20\ \text{L/min}$: the tank loses 20 L every minute.
:::

:::summary
- Linear relation = constant rate of change = straight-line graph.
- Rate of change $= \Delta y / \Delta x$, with units.
- Tables: check $\Delta y / \Delta x$ between rows. Equations: variables to the first power only.
:::
