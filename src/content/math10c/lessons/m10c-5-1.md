A graph tells a story. A flat stretch means nothing is changing; a steep climb means something is changing quickly. In this lesson you will read the story in a graph, draw a graph from a story, and decide when points should be joined.

## Reading a graph

When the horizontal axis is **time**, the shape of a graph describes how a quantity changes:

| Shape of a segment | Meaning |
|---|---|
| rises from left to right | the quantity is increasing |
| falls from left to right | the quantity is decreasing |
| horizontal | the quantity is not changing |
| steeper | changing faster |

:::example Telling the story
The graph shows Nadia’s distance from home during a walk. Describe each segment.
@step **A:** she walks away from home, $600\ \text{m}$ in $10$ min ($60\ \text{m/min}$).
@step **B:** the graph is flat for $6$ min — she stopped.
@step **C:** she moves away again, $900\ \text{m}$ in $6$ min ($150\ \text{m/min}$) — the steepest segment, so the fastest.
@step **D:** the distance falls to 0 — she returns home, $1500\ \text{m}$ in $18$ min.
:::

:::mistake A distance–time graph is not a map
A segment going “down” does not mean walking downhill. It means the distance from home is decreasing.

## Sketching a graph from a story

Break the story into parts. For each part ask: increasing, decreasing or constant? Fast or slow? Then draw connected segments.

:::example Filling a bathtub
A tub fills at a steady rate, stays full while someone bathes, then drains quickly. Sketch volume against time.
@step Filling: a straight segment rising.
@step Bathing: a horizontal segment.
@step Draining quickly: a steep segment falling to 0.
:::

## Continuous or discrete?

:::definition Continuous data
Data that can take **any** value in an interval, like time, distance or temperature. Join the points.
:::

:::definition Discrete data
Data that can only take **separate** values, often whole numbers, like the number of tickets sold or people in a room. Do **not** join the points.
:::

:::example To join or not to join?
(a) The cost of buying $n$ movie tickets. (b) The height of a plant over several days.
@step (a) You cannot buy $2.5$ tickets: discrete — plot separate points.
@step (b) The plant grows between measurements: continuous — join the points.
:::

:::summary
- Up = increasing, down = decreasing, flat = no change; steeper = faster.
- To sketch, break a story into parts and describe each part’s direction and speed.
- Join points for continuous data; leave them separate for discrete data.
:::
