Real data is messy: points rarely lie exactly on a line. But if they follow a straight-line trend, a **line of best fit** captures the pattern and lets you make predictions.

## Scatterplots and correlation

:::definition Scatterplot
A graph of paired data points that shows whether two variables are related.
:::

- **Positive correlation:** as $x$ increases, $y$ tends to increase.
- **Negative correlation:** as $x$ increases, $y$ tends to decrease.
- **No correlation:** no clear pattern.

:::

## Drawing a line of best fit

A line of best fit:

- follows the direction of the data,
- has roughly the same number of points above and below it,
- does not have to pass through any data point.

Graphing technology calculates the best-fitting line exactly (linear regression). By hand, place a ruler along the trend and adjust it until the points are balanced on both sides.

## Making predictions

:::definition Interpolation and extrapolation
**Interpolation** predicts a value **within** the range of the data. **Extrapolation** predicts **beyond** it. Extrapolation is less reliable, because the trend may not continue.
:::

:::example Predicting
Using $y = x + 2$ for data collected from $x = 1$ to $x = 8$, predict $y$ when $x = 5.5$ and when $x = 15$.
@step $x = 5.5$: $y = 7.5$ (interpolation — reliable)
@step $x = 15$: $y = 17$ (extrapolation — use with caution)
:::

:::check r7-best-fit 3
:::

:::mistake Joining the dots instead of fitting a trend
A line of best fit does **not** have to pass through the first and last points, or through any point. Draw a straight line that follows the overall trend with roughly as many points above it as below it.
:::

:::mistake Predicting far outside the data
Predictions **between** data values (interpolation) are usually reliable; predictions far **beyond** them (extrapolation) may not be. A model of a child's height from age 3 to 10 should not be used to predict their height at age 40.
:::

:::summary
- Scatterplots show positive, negative or no correlation.
- A line of best fit follows the trend with points balanced above and below.
- Use two points on the line to write its equation.
- Interpolation is within the data; extrapolation is beyond it and less reliable.
:::
