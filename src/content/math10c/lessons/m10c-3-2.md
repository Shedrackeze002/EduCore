Is 1764 a perfect square? What is $\sqrt[3]{2744}$? You can answer both without a calculator once you have the prime factorization.

## Perfect squares and perfect cubes

:::definition Perfect square
A number that is the square of a whole number, like $144 = 12^2$. A square with whole-number side length has this area.
:::

:::definition Perfect cube
A number that is the cube of a whole number, like $216 = 6^3$. A cube with whole-number edge length has this volume.
:::

Look at the prime factorizations:

$$144 = 2^4 \times 3^2 \qquad 216 = 2^3 \times 3^3 \qquad 64 = 2^6$$

- In a **perfect square**, every exponent is **even** (divisible by 2).
- In a **perfect cube**, every exponent is **divisible by 3**.
- $64 = 2^6$ is both, because 6 is divisible by 2 and by 3.

:::example Square, cube, both or neither?
Classify $1728$, $900$ and $200$.
@step $1728 = 2^6 \times 3^3$: exponents 6 and 3 are divisible by 3, but 3 is odd. **Perfect cube only** ($12^3$).
@step $900 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5^2$: all exponents even, not all divisible by 3. **Perfect square only** ($30^2$).
@step $200 = 2^3 \times 5^2$: exponent 3 is odd and exponent 2 is not divisible by 3. **Neither**.
:::

## Square roots and cube roots

The square root splits the prime factors into **two equal groups**; the cube root splits them into **three equal groups**. Each group is the root.

:::example Square root by prime factors
Find $\sqrt{1764}$.
@step $1764 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 7^2$
@step Two equal groups: $(2 \times 3 \times 7)(2 \times 3 \times 7)$
@answer $\sqrt{1764} = 2 \times 3 \times 7 = 42$
:::

:::example Cube root by prime factors
Find $\sqrt[3]{2744}$.
@step $2744 = 2^3 \times 7^3$
@step Three equal groups of $2 \times 7$
@answer $\sqrt[3]{2744} = 14$
:::

:::tip Halve or third the exponents
For a square root, halve every exponent: $\sqrt{2^4 \times 3^2} = 2^2 \times 3 = 12$. For a cube root, divide every exponent by 3.
:::

:::tip Negative numbers
$(-4)^3 = -64$, so $\sqrt[3]{-64} = -4$. A cube root of a negative number is negative. But no real number squared is negative, so $\sqrt{-64}$ is not a real number.
:::

## Problems with squares and cubes

:::example Edge length from volume
A cube-shaped box has a volume of $3375\ \text{cm}^3$. Find its edge length.
@step $3375 = 3^3 \times 5^3$
@answer $s = \sqrt[3]{3375} = 3 \times 5 = 15\ \text{cm}$
:::

:::example From surface area to volume
A cube has surface area $294\ \text{cm}^2$. Find its volume.
@step Each of the 6 faces: $294 \div 6 = 49\ \text{cm}^2$
@step Edge: $\sqrt{49} = 7\ \text{cm}$
@answer $V = 7^3 = 343\ \text{cm}^3$
:::

:::summary
- Perfect square: all exponents in the prime factorization are even. Perfect cube: all exponents divisible by 3.
- Square root: split primes into two equal groups (halve exponents). Cube root: three equal groups (divide exponents by 3).
- Cube roots of negative numbers are negative; square roots of negative numbers are not real.
:::
