Two lines in a plane can cross once, never meet, or lie on top of each other. That means a system of two linear equations has exactly **one**, **no**, or **infinitely many** solutions. In this lesson you will tell which from the equations, and solve a range of problems with systems.

## Three possibilities

| Lines | Slopes and intercepts | Number of solutions |
|---|---|---|
| intersect | different slopes | **one** |
| parallel and distinct | same slope, different $y$-intercepts | **none** |
| coincident (same line) | same slope, same $y$-intercept | **infinitely many** |

:::example Classifying systems
How many solutions does each system have?

(a) $\begin{cases} y = 2x + 1 \\ y = -x + 4 \end{cases}$ (b) $\begin{cases} 2x - y = 3 \\ 4x - 2y = 10 \end{cases}$ (c) $\begin{cases} x + 3y = 6 \\ 2x + 6y = 12 \end{cases}$

@step (a) Slopes $2$ and $-1$ are different: **one solution**.
@step (b) $y = 2x - 3$ and $y = 2x - 5$: same slope, different intercepts: **no solution**.
@step (c) The second equation is the first multiplied by 2: the same line, **infinitely many solutions**.
:::

:::tip What algebra shows
If elimination gives a false statement like $0 = 4$, there is no solution. If it gives a true statement like $0 = 0$, there are infinitely many.
:::

## Problem solving with systems

1. **Define** two variables clearly (with units).
2. **Write** two equations from the information.
3. **Solve** with the most efficient method.
4. **Check** the solution in the original words of the problem.
5. **Answer** in a sentence.

:::example Tickets
150 tickets were sold. Adult tickets cost \$12, student tickets \$8, and sales totalled \$1480. How many of each?
@step $a + s = 150$ and $12a + 8s = 1480$
@step Multiply the first by $-8$: $-8a - 8s = -1200$. Add: $4a = 280$, so $a = 70$
@step $s = 150 - 70 = 80$
@answer 70 adult and 80 student tickets. Check: $70 \times 12 + 80 \times 8 = 1480$ ✓
:::

:::example Comparing plans
Rental A costs \$40 plus \$0.25/km. Rental B costs \$70 plus \$0.15/km. When do they cost the same?
@step $C = 0.25d + 40$ and $C = 0.15d + 70$
@step $0.25d + 40 = 0.15d + 70$, so $0.10d = 30$ and $d = 300$
@step $C = 0.25(300) + 40 = 115$
@answer Both cost \$115 for 300 km. Below 300 km, A is cheaper; above, B is cheaper.
:::

:::example A rectangle
A rectangle’s length is 7 cm more than its width, and its perimeter is 54 cm. Find its dimensions.
@step $l = w + 7$ and $2l + 2w = 54$
@step $2(w + 7) + 2w = 54$, so $4w = 40$ and $w = 10$
@answer Width $10\ \text{cm}$, length $17\ \text{cm}$.
:::

:::summary
- Different slopes → one solution; parallel → none; coincident → infinitely many.
- In algebra, $0 = \text{non-zero}$ means none, $0 = 0$ means infinitely many.
- Define variables, write two equations, solve, check, and answer in context.
:::
