Would you take a job that pays 1 cent on the first day, 2 cents on the second, 4 cents on the third, doubling every day for 30 days? The total pay is $2^{30} - 1 = 1\,073\,741\,823$ cents — more than \$10.7 million. Adding the terms of a geometric sequence like this is what geometric series are about.

## The sum formula

:::definition Geometric series
The sum of the terms of a geometric sequence: $S_n = t_1 + t_1 r + t_1 r^2 + \cdots + t_1 r^{n-1}$.
:::

Here is why the formula works. Multiply the sum by $r$, line the two sums up, and subtract:

$$\begin{aligned} rS_n &= t_1 r + t_1 r^2 + \cdots + t_1 r^{n-1} + t_1 r^n \\ S_n &= t_1 + t_1 r + t_1 r^2 + \cdots + t_1 r^{n-1} \\ rS_n - S_n &= t_1 r^n - t_1 \end{aligned}$$

All the middle terms cancel. Factor: $S_n(r - 1) = t_1(r^n - 1)$, then divide by $r - 1$.

:::definition Sum of a geometric series
For $r \ne 1$:
$$S_n = \frac{t_1(r^n - 1)}{r - 1} \qquad\text{or}\qquad S_n = \frac{rt_n - t_1}{r - 1}$$
The second form uses the last term, because $t_1 r^n = r\,t_n$.
:::

If $r = 1$, every term equals $t_1$ and $S_n = nt_1$.

:::tip Ratios between 0 and 1
Multiplying the top and bottom by $-1$ gives $S_n = \dfrac{t_1(1 - r^n)}{1 - r}$. It is the same formula, but with $0 < r < 1$ both brackets are positive, which is easier to check.
:::

## Finding sums

:::example A positive ratio
Find the sum of the first 8 terms of $3 + 6 + 12 + \cdots$
@step $t_1 = 3$, $r = 2$, $n = 8$
@step $S_8 = \dfrac{3(2^8 - 1)}{2 - 1} = \dfrac{3(255)}{1}$
@answer $S_8 = 765$
:::

:::example A negative ratio
Find the sum of the first 9 terms of $5 - 10 + 20 - \cdots$
@step $t_1 = 5$, $r = -2$, $n = 9$
@step $S_9 = \dfrac{5\left((-2)^9 - 1\right)}{-2 - 1} = \dfrac{5(-513)}{-3}$
@answer $S_9 = 855$
:::

:::example When the last term is given
Find the sum of $2 + 6 + 18 + \cdots + 4374$.
@step $t_1 = 2$, $r = 3$, $t_n = 4374$
@step $S_n = \dfrac{rt_n - t_1}{r - 1} = \dfrac{3(4374) - 2}{3 - 1} = \dfrac{13\,120}{2}$
@answer The sum is $6560$. (Check: $4374 = 2(3)^7$, so $n = 8$ and $\dfrac{2(3^8 - 1)}{2} = 6560$.)
:::

:::mistake Using the wrong power
The sum formula uses $r^n$, while the general term uses $r^{n-1}$. Mixing them up gives the sum of one term too few. Also keep $(-2)^9$ in brackets: it is $-512$.
:::

## Problems with geometric series

A geometric series appears whenever a total is built from amounts that grow or shrink by the same factor: emails forwarded in rounds, salaries with a yearly percentage raise, or the heights of a bouncing ball.

:::example A salary with raises
A geologist earns \$50,000 in the first year, and the salary rises by 3% each year. What is the total earned over 10 years?
@step $t_1 = 50\,000$, $r = 1.03$, $n = 10$
@step $S_{10} = \dfrac{50\,000(1.03^{10} - 1)}{1.03 - 1} \approx 573\,193.97$
@answer The total is about \$573,193.97.
:::

:::example Total distance of a bouncing ball
The ball in the diagram is dropped from 4 m and rebounds to 75% of its height each time. How far has it travelled when it hits the ground for the 5th time?
@step First drop: 4 m. Before the 5th hit there are 4 bounces: $3 + 2.25 + 1.6875 + 1.265625$.
@step $S_4 = \dfrac{3(1 - 0.75^4)}{1 - 0.75} = 8.203125$
@step Each bounce goes up and comes down: $4 + 2(8.203125) = 20.40625$
@answer The ball has travelled about $20.4$ m.
:::

:::mistake Counting the bounces
Draw the path. The first drop happens once; every bounce after it is travelled **twice**. Before the $k$th hit there are only $k - 1$ bounces.
:::

:::summary
- Multiply by $r$ and subtract to prove $S_n = \dfrac{t_1(r^n - 1)}{r - 1}$ for $r \ne 1$.
- With the last term: $S_n = \dfrac{rt_n - t_1}{r - 1}$, so you do not need $n$.
- Bracket negative ratios and round only at the end.
- Percentage growth or decay each period makes a geometric series with $r = 1 \pm \frac{p}{100}$.
- For a bouncing ball, add the first drop to twice the sum of the bounce heights.
:::
