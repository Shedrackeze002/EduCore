The perimeter of a garden, the area of a rink, the points a team earns in a season: algebra lets you write all of these as expressions like $2x + 6$ or $x^2 + 3x$. Expressions like these are called **polynomials**. In this lesson you will learn the names of their parts, so you can talk about them clearly.

## Terms, coefficients and constants

Look at the expression $5x^2 - 3x + 8$. It is built from three pieces, joined by $+$ and $-$ signs.

:::definition Term
A **term** is a number, a variable, or a number multiplied by variables. In $5x^2 - 3x + 8$ the terms are $5x^2$, $-3x$ and $8$.
:::

:::definition Coefficient
The **coefficient** of a term is the number that multiplies the variable. In $5x^2$ the coefficient is $5$. In $-3x$ the coefficient is $-3$.
:::

:::definition Constant term
A **constant term** is a term with no variable. Its value never changes. In $5x^2 - 3x + 8$ the constant term is $8$.
:::

:::example Name the parts
For $4x^2 - x + 7$, list the terms, the coefficient of $x^2$, the coefficient of $x$ and the constant term.
@step The terms are separated by $+$ and $-$ signs: $4x^2$, $-x$ and $7$.
@step The coefficient of $x^2$ is $4$.
@step $-x$ means $-1x$, so the coefficient of $x$ is $-1$.
@step The term with no variable is $7$.
@answer Terms: $4x^2$, $-x$, $7$. Coefficients: $4$ and $-1$. Constant term: $7$.
:::

:::mistake The sign belongs to the term
In $4x^2 - x + 7$ the middle term is $-x$, not $x$. Always keep the sign in front of a term with it. Also remember that $x$ means $1x$ and $-x$ means $-1x$, so their coefficients are $1$ and $-1$.
:::

## Polynomials and their degree

:::definition Polynomial
A **polynomial** is one term or a sum of terms. Each term is a number, or a number times variables with whole-number exponents. $3x^2 - 2x + 1$, $4xy$ and $7 - x$ are polynomials.
:::

:::definition Degree
The **degree of a term** is the sum of the exponents on its variables. $5x^2$ has degree $2$, $-3x$ has degree $1$, and a constant like $8$ has degree $0$. The **degree of a polynomial** is the highest degree of any of its terms.
:::

In Grade 9 you will work with polynomials of degree $2$ or less.

:::example Find the degree
What is the degree of $7 - 4x + x^2$?
@step The terms are $7$, $-4x$ and $x^2$.
@step Their degrees are $0$, $1$ and $2$.
@answer The highest degree is $2$, so the polynomial has degree $2$.
:::

:::example A term with two variables
What is the degree of $3xy - 5x + 2$?
@step $3xy$ means $3x^1y^1$. Add the exponents: $1 + 1 = 2$.
@step $-5x$ has degree $1$ and $2$ has degree $0$.
@answer The highest degree is $2$, so the polynomial has degree $2$.
:::

:::mistake Degree is not the number of terms
$x^2 + 1$ has two terms, but its degree is $2$ because of the exponent on $x^2$. And $6x - 1$ has two terms with degree $1$. Count terms for the type, look at exponents for the degree.
:::

## Monomials, binomials and trinomials

Polynomials are named by how many terms they have.

:::definition Monomial, binomial, trinomial
A **monomial** has one term, like $-6x^2$. A **binomial** has two terms, like $2x + 5$. A **trinomial** has three terms, like $x^2 - 4x + 3$.
:::

Polynomials are usually written in **descending order**: the term with the highest degree first and the constant term last.

:::example Classify and reorder
Classify $6 - 2x^2 + x$ and write it in descending order.
@step The terms are $6$, $-2x^2$ and $x$. There are $3$ terms, so it is a trinomial.
@step Put the $x^2$-term first, then the $x$-term, then the constant. Each term keeps its sign.
@answer It is a trinomial. In descending order it is $-2x^2 + x + 6$.
:::

:::tip Count before you classify
Count the terms in the expression as it is written, after any like terms are combined. Never count the signs: $-2x^2 + x + 6$ has two signs between terms but three terms.
:::

:::summary
- A term is a number, a variable, or a product of numbers and variables. The sign in front of a term belongs to it.
- The coefficient is the number multiplying the variables: $x$ has coefficient $1$ and $-x$ has coefficient $-1$.
- The constant term has no variable.
- The degree of a term is the sum of its exponents; the degree of a polynomial is the highest degree of its terms.
- One term: monomial. Two terms: binomial. Three terms: trinomial.
:::
