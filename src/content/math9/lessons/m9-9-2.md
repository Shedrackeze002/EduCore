A ceiling fan, a hubcap and a snowflake look the same after a part turn. They have rotation symmetry, a different kind of balance from a fold line.

## Rotation symmetry

:::definition Rotation symmetry
A shape has **rotation symmetry** when it fits onto itself after a turn of less than $360^\circ$ about a point. That point is the **centre of rotation**.
:::

:::definition Order of rotation
The **order of rotation** is the number of times a shape fits onto itself during one full turn. A shape that fits onto itself only after a full $360^\circ$ turn has **no** rotation symmetry.
:::

:::example The pinwheel
What is the order of rotation of the pinwheel?
@step It fits onto itself after turns of $90^\circ$, $180^\circ$, $270^\circ$ and $360^\circ$.
@step That is $4$ times in one full turn.
@answer The order of rotation is $4$.
:::

:::mistake Counting the start twice
The starting position and the position after a full turn are the same. Count the full turn once, and do not count the start as well.
:::

## The angle of rotation symmetry

:::definition Angle of rotation symmetry
The **angle of rotation symmetry** is the smallest turn that makes the shape fit onto itself:
$$\text{angle} = 360^\circ \div \text{order}$$
:::

:::example A hexagon logo
The logo is a regular hexagon with a coloured triangle inside. Find its order and angle of rotation symmetry.
@step The hexagon alone fits onto itself $6$ times, but the triangle only matches after every second turn.
@step The whole logo fits onto itself after $120^\circ$, $240^\circ$ and $360^\circ$: order $3$.
@answer Order $3$; angle $= 360^\circ \div 3 = 120^\circ$.
:::

You can also work backward: a design that first fits onto itself after $45^\circ$ has order $360^\circ \div 45^\circ = 8$.

:::check m9-rotation-symmetry 2
:::

## Describing the symmetry of a design

A design can have line symmetry, rotation symmetry, both, or neither.

Describe the symmetry of this block letter S.
@step No fold line makes the two halves match, so it has no line symmetry.
@step After a half turn ($180^\circ$) it fits onto itself, so it has rotation symmetry of order $2$.
@answer The letter S has rotation symmetry only.

:::example A two-colour decal
Describe the symmetry of this decal. The colours must match too.
@step A vertical fold and a horizontal fold both work: $2$ lines of symmetry.
@step A quarter turn puts each petal where a petal of the other colour was, so it does not work. A half turn does: order $2$.
@answer The decal has both line symmetry and rotation symmetry.
:::

:::tip Two lines mean a turn
If a shape has two or more lines of symmetry, it always has rotation symmetry too. A shape can still have rotation symmetry with no lines of symmetry, like the pinwheel.
:::

:::check m9-rotation-symmetry 3
:::

:::summary
- A shape has rotation symmetry if it fits onto itself after a turn of less than $360^\circ$.
- The order of rotation is how many times it fits onto itself in one full turn.
- Angle of rotation symmetry $= 360^\circ \div \text{order}$.
- A design can have line symmetry only, rotation symmetry only, both, or neither. Colours must match too.
:::
