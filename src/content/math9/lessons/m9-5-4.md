A rectangular rink is $2x$ metres wide and $(3x - 5)$ metres long. What is its area? To answer, you multiply a polynomial by a monomial. In this lesson you will multiply and divide polynomials by monomials, using area models and the distributive property.

## Multiplying monomials

Think of a rectangle that is $3x$ wide and $2x$ long. Its area is $3x \times 2x$. Multiply the numbers and multiply the variables: $3 \times 2 = 6$ and $x \times x = x^2$.

:::figure
{"type":"areaModel","top":["2x"],"side":["3x"],"cells":[["6x^2"]]}
:::

:::definition Multiplying monomials
To multiply monomials, multiply the coefficients, then multiply the variables: $x \cdot x = x^2$ and $x \cdot y = xy$.
:::

:::example Products of monomials
Find $(-4x)(5x)$, $(3x)(-2y)$ and $(-2)(-7x)$.
@step $(-4)(5) = -20$ and $x \cdot x = x^2$, so $(-4x)(5x) = -20x^2$.
@step $(3)(-2) = -6$ and $x \cdot y = xy$, so $(3x)(-2y) = -6xy$.
@step $(-2)(-7) = 14$, so $(-2)(-7x) = 14x$.
@answer $-20x^2$, $-6xy$ and $14x$
:::

## Multiplying a polynomial by a monomial

:::definition Distributive property
The **distributive property** says $a(b + c) = ab + ac$. The number outside the brackets multiplies **every** term inside.
:::

An area model shows why. A rectangle that is $2x$ by $(3x - 5)$ splits into two parts.

:::figure
{"type":"areaModel","top":["3x","-5"],"side":["2x"],"cells":[["6x^2","-10x"]]}
:::

:::example Use an area model
Expand $2x(3x - 5)$.
@step Multiply $2x$ by the first term: $2x \cdot 3x = 6x^2$
@step Multiply $2x$ by the second term: $2x \cdot (-5) = -10x$
@answer $2x(3x - 5) = 6x^2 - 10x$
:::

:::example A negative monomial
Expand $-3(x^2 + 2x - 1)$.
@step $-3 \cdot x^2 = -3x^2$
@step $-3 \cdot 2x = -6x$
@step $-3 \cdot (-1) = 3$
@answer $-3x^2 - 6x + 3$
:::

:::mistake Multiplying only the first term
$2x(3x - 5)$ is **not** $6x^2 - 5$. The $2x$ multiplies both terms. Watch the signs too: in $-3(x^2 + 2x - 1)$ the last product is $(-3)(-1) = +3$.
:::

:::

Some expressions need two products and then like terms.

:::example Expand and simplify
Expand and simplify $2x(x + 3) - 4(x - 1)$.
@step First product: $2x(x + 3) = 2x^2 + 6x$
@step Second product: $-4(x - 1) = -4x + 4$. The $-4$ multiplies both terms.
@step Collect like terms: $2x^2 + 6x - 4x + 4$
@answer $2x^2 + 2x + 4$
:::

:::

## Dividing a polynomial by a monomial

Division undoes multiplication. If a rectangle has area $6x^2 + 9x$ and one side is $3x$, the other side is $(6x^2 + 9x) \div 3x$.

:::figure
{"type":"areaModel","top":["?","?"],"side":["3x"],"cells":[["6x^2","9x"]]}
:::

To divide a polynomial by a monomial, divide **each term** by the monomial. Divide the coefficients, then the variables: $\dfrac{x^2}{x} = x$ and $\dfrac{x}{x} = 1$.

:::example Find the missing side
Divide $(6x^2 + 9x) \div (3x)$.
@step Divide each term: $\dfrac{6x^2}{3x} + \dfrac{9x}{3x}$
@step $\dfrac{6x^2}{3x} = 2x$ and $\dfrac{9x}{3x} = 3$
@step Check by multiplying: $3x(2x + 3) = 6x^2 + 9x$
@answer $2x + 3$
:::

:::example Dividing by a negative monomial
Divide $\dfrac{-12x^2 + 8x}{-4x}$.
@step $\dfrac{-12x^2}{-4x} = 3x$, because $(-12) \div (-4) = 3$.
@step $\dfrac{8x}{-4x} = -2$
@step Check: $-4x(3x - 2) = -12x^2 + 8x$
@answer $3x - 2$
:::

:::mistake Dividing only one term
$\dfrac{6x^2 + 9x}{3x}$ is **not** $2x + 9x$. Every term on top is divided by $3x$. Also, $\dfrac{9x}{3x}$ is $3$, not $3x$: the $x$ divides out.
:::

:::summary
- To multiply monomials, multiply the coefficients and then the variables: $x \cdot x = x^2$.
- To multiply a polynomial by a monomial, multiply **every** term by the monomial (the distributive property). An area model keeps track of the products.
- To divide a polynomial by a monomial, divide **every** term by the monomial.
- Check a division by multiplying the answer by the monomial.
:::
