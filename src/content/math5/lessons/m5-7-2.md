A pack of hockey cards has $8$ cards. $3$ packs have $8 \times 3 = 24$ cards. But what if you do not know how many packs there are? In algebra, a letter can stand for a number you do not know yet.

## Variables and algebraic expressions

:::definition Variable
A **variable** is a letter, such as $n$, that stands for a number you do not know yet.
:::

:::definition Algebraic expression
An **algebraic expression** has numbers, operations and at least one variable, such as $8n$ or $\dfrac{n}{4} + 2$.
:::

Adding the same variable again and again is multiplying: $n + n + n = 3 \times n$. We write a product with a variable **without** the $\times$ sign, number first: $3n$. We write a quotient with a variable as a **fraction**: $n \div 4 = \dfrac{n}{4}$.

:::example Write it the algebra way
Write $7 \times k$ and $k \div 2$ the algebra way.
@step A product: put the number right beside the letter, number first. $7 \times k = 7k$.
@step A quotient: write a fraction, with the number being divided on top. $k \div 2 = \dfrac{k}{2}$.
@answer $7k$ and $\dfrac{k}{2}$
:::

:::mistake 4n is a product
$4n$ means $4 \times n$. It does not mean $4 + n$. If $n = 6$, then $4n = 24$, not $46$.
:::

## Terms, coefficients and constant terms

:::definition Term
A **term** is a product of a number and a variable (like $5p$), a quotient of a variable and a number (like $\dfrac{p}{3}$), or a number on its own (like $8$). Terms are joined by $+$ or $-$ signs.
:::

:::definition Coefficient and constant term
In the term $5p$, the number $5$ is the **coefficient**. A number on its own, like $8$, is a **constant term**.
:::

:::example Name the parts
Name the terms, the coefficient and the constant term of $5p + 8$.
@step The $+$ sign joins two terms: $5p$ and $8$.
@step In $5p$, the number multiplied by $p$ is $5$. That is the coefficient.
@answer Terms: $5p$ and $8$. Coefficient: $5$. Constant term: $8$.
:::

## Writing an expression for a situation

Read the situation carefully. Each word gives a clue:

- "each" or "per" means **multiply** the variable;
- "shared equally" or "cut into equal pieces" means **divide** the variable;
- an amount that is added only once is a **constant term**.

:::example Tickets and parking
Movie tickets cost \$9 each. Parking costs \$5. Write an expression for the total cost, in dollars, of $t$ tickets and parking.
@step Each ticket costs \$9, so $t$ tickets cost $9t$ dollars.
@step Parking is paid once, so add $5$.
@answer $9t + 5$
:::

:::example Sharing crackers
Mei shares $c$ crackers equally among $4$ friends. Write an expression for the number of crackers each friend gets.
@step Sharing equally means dividing: $c \div 4$.
@answer $\dfrac{c}{4}$
:::

## Evaluating an expression

To **evaluate** an expression, **substitute** (put in) a number for the variable. Then follow the order of operations.

:::example Substitute and multiply first
Evaluate $4n + 3$ when $n = 6$.
@step Substitute $n = 6$: $4 \times 6 + 3$.
@step Multiply first: $4 \times 6 = 24$.
@step Then add: $24 + 3 = 27$.
@answer The value is $27$.
:::

:::example A quotient
Evaluate $\dfrac{m}{5} + 7$ when $m = 30$.
@step Substitute $m = 30$: $\dfrac{30}{5} + 7$.
@step Divide first: $30 \div 5 = 6$. Then add: $6 + 7 = 13$.
@answer The value is $13$.
:::

:::mistake Put the times sign back
When you substitute into $4n$, write $4 \times 6$. Writing the digits side by side gives $46$, which is wrong.
:::

:::summary
- A variable is a letter that stands for an unknown number.
- Write $3 \times n$ as $3n$ and $n \div 4$ as $\dfrac{n}{4}$.
- In $5p + 8$, the terms are $5p$ and $8$, the coefficient is $5$ and the constant term is $8$.
- "Each" means multiply, "shared equally" means divide, and an amount added once is a constant term.
- To evaluate, substitute the number for the variable, then follow the order of operations.
:::
