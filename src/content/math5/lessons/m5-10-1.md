How much paper covers a book? How much carpet covers a floor? How big is a lake? Each question is about area, and each one needs a different unit.

## What is area?

:::definition Area
**Area** is the amount of surface a shape covers. We measure area by counting how many squares of one size cover the shape, with no gaps and no overlaps.
:::

:::definition Square centimetre
A **square centimetre** ($\text{cm}^2$) is the area of a square that is $1\ \text{cm}$ long on each side. It is about the size of the tip of your finger.
:::

A centimetre ($\text{cm}$) measures a length. A square centimetre ($\text{cm}^2$) measures an area. On centimetre grid paper, each small square is $1\ \text{cm}^2$.

:::example Counting squares
Each small square is $1\ \text{cm}^2$. What is the area of the L shape?
@step The bottom two rows have $4$ squares each: $4 + 4 = 8$.
@step The top two rows have $2$ squares each: $2 + 2 = 4$.
@answer The area is $8 + 4 = 12\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::

:::mistake cm or cm²?
Lengths use $\text{cm}$ or $\text{m}$. Areas use **square** units: $\text{cm}^2$, $\text{m}^2$ or $\text{km}^2$. "12 cm" is a length, but "12 cm²" is an area.
:::

## Square metres and square kilometres

:::definition Square metre
A **square metre** ($\text{m}^2$) is the area of a square that is $1\ \text{m}$ long on each side. Four metre sticks can make the outline of one. It is about the size of a small table top.
:::

:::definition Square kilometre
A **square kilometre** ($\text{km}^2$) is the area of a square that is $1\ \text{km}$ long on each side. It takes about 15 minutes to walk along one side.
:::

Choose the unit that fits the size of the thing:

:::figure

:::

:::example Choosing a unit
Which unit is best for the area of a hockey rink?
@step A hockey rink is much bigger than your hand, so $\text{cm}^2$ would give a huge number.
@step You can walk across it in a minute, so it is much smaller than $1\ \text{km}^2$.
@answer Use square metres ($\text{m}^2$).
:::

## How the units are related

A square metre is $100\ \text{cm}$ long on each side. Picture it split into $100$ rows of $100$ square centimetres. In the picture, each small square is $10\ \text{cm}$ by $10\ \text{cm}$, so it holds $100$ square centimetres.

:::figure
{"type":"plane","grid":{"xMin":0,"xMax":10,"yMin":0,"yMax":10,"step":1},"hideTickLabels":true,"hideAxes":true,"polylines":[{"points":[[0,0],[10,0],[10,10],[0,10],[0,0]],"fill":true}],"caption":"One square metre split into 100 squares, each 10 cm by 10 cm"}
:::

$$1\ \text{m}^2 = 100 \times 100 = 10\,000\ \text{cm}^2$$

A square kilometre is $1\,000\ \text{m}$ long on each side, so

$$1\ \text{km}^2 = 1\,000 \times 1\,000 = 1\,000\,000\ \text{m}^2$$

:::mistake 1 m² is not 100 cm²
$1\ \text{m} = 100\ \text{cm}$, but $1\ \text{m}^2$ is **not** $100\ \text{cm}^2$. A square metre has $100$ rows of $100$ square centimetres, which is $10\,000\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::

:::example Changing units
A rug has an area of $3\ \text{m}^2$. What is its area in square centimetres?
@step Each square metre is $10\,000\ \text{cm}^2$.
@step $3 \times 10\,000 = 30\,000$
@answer The rug has an area of $30\,000\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::

:::example Back to a bigger unit
A forest has an area of $4\,000\,000\ \text{m}^2$. What is its area in square kilometres?
@step Each square kilometre is $1\,000\,000\ \text{m}^2$.
@step $4\,000\,000\ \text{m}^2$ is $4$ groups of $1\,000\,000\ \text{m}^2$.
@answer The forest has an area of $4\ \text{km}^2$.
:::

## Estimating area

To estimate an area, compare it with a benchmark. A door is about as tall as two metre sticks and about one metre wide, so its area is about $2\ \text{m}^2$.

On grid paper, a shape with slanted sides cuts some squares in half. Count the whole squares, then count the half squares. Two halves make one whole.

:::example Whole and half squares
Each small square is $1\ \text{cm}^2$. What is the area of the triangle?
@step There are $6$ whole squares inside the triangle.
@step The slanted side cuts $4$ squares in half. $4$ halves make $4 \div 2 = 2$ whole squares.
@answer The area is $6 + 2 = 8\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::

:::summary
- Area is the amount of surface a shape covers. It is measured in square units.
- $1\ \text{cm}^2$ is a square $1\ \text{cm}$ by $1\ \text{cm}$; $1\ \text{m}^2$ is a square $1\ \text{m}$ by $1\ \text{m}$; $1\ \text{km}^2$ is a square $1\ \text{km}$ by $1\ \text{km}$.
- Use $\text{cm}^2$ for small things, $\text{m}^2$ for rooms and fields, and $\text{km}^2$ for very large places.
- $1\ \text{m}^2 = 10\,000\ \text{cm}^2$ and $1\ \text{km}^2 = 1\,000\,000\ \text{m}^2$.
- Estimate with benchmarks, or count whole squares and half squares on grid paper.
:::
