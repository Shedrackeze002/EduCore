:::definition Mixed number
A **mixed number** has a whole-number part and a fraction part, such as \dfrac{3}{4}$. It means  + \dfrac{3}{4}$.
:::

To change an improper fraction to a mixed number, **divide** the numerator by the denominator.

:::definition Benchmark
A **benchmark** is a friendly number that we compare other numbers to. For fractions we use /bin/bash$, $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $.
:::

- If the numerator is **half** of the denominator, the fraction is $\dfrac{1}{2}$, like $\dfrac{4}{8}$.

- An improper fraction, like $\dfrac{7}{4}$, has a numerator greater than its denominator. It is more than $.
