Hex nuts, octagonal signs, gazebo floors and many floor tiles are **regular polygons**. To cut, build or check one of these shapes, you need its angles. A few simple rules give every angle of a regular polygon from its number of sides.

## The interior angle sum

Pick one corner of a convex polygon and draw every diagonal from it. A polygon with $n$ sides splits into $n - 2$ triangles, and each triangle holds $180^\circ$.

The interior angles of a convex polygon with $n$ sides add to
$$180^\circ(n - 2)$$

## Interior angles of regular polygons

:::definition Regular polygon
A **regular polygon** has all sides equal and all angles equal. Each **interior angle** is
$$\dfrac{180^\circ(n - 2)}{n}$$
:::

:::example A hex nut
A hex nut is a regular hexagon. What is each interior angle?
@step Angle sum: $180^\circ(6 - 2) = 720^\circ$.
@step Six equal angles: $720^\circ \div 6 = 120^\circ$.
@answer Each interior angle is $120^\circ$.
:::

:::mistake Dividing $360^\circ$ for the interior angle
$360^\circ \div 6 = 60^\circ$ is the **exterior** angle (or the angle at the centre), not the interior angle. A hexagon's corners are clearly wider than $90^\circ$, so $60^\circ$ cannot be right.
:::

## Exterior angles

:::definition Exterior angle
Extend one side of a polygon past a corner. The angle between the extension and the next side is an **exterior angle**. At each corner, the interior angle and the exterior angle add to $180^\circ$.
:::

Walk all the way around a polygon: at each corner you turn through its exterior angle, and you make exactly one full turn. So the exterior angles of any convex polygon add to $360^\circ$, and each exterior angle of a regular polygon is $\dfrac{360^\circ}{n}$.

:::example An octagonal sign
For a regular octagon, find each exterior angle and each interior angle.
@step Exterior: $360^\circ \div 8 = 45^\circ$.
@step Interior: $180^\circ - 45^\circ = 135^\circ$. (Check: $\dfrac{180^\circ(8 - 2)}{8} = \dfrac{1080^\circ}{8} = 135^\circ$.)
@answer Each exterior angle is $45^\circ$ and each interior angle is $135^\circ$.
:::

## Finding the number of sides

Work backwards from an angle:

- From an exterior angle: $n = 360^\circ \div$ (exterior angle).
- From an interior angle: find the exterior angle first, $180^\circ -$ (interior angle), then divide $360^\circ$ by it.
- From the angle sum: solve $180(n - 2) = $ sum.

:::example Three ways to find n
(a) Each exterior angle is $24^\circ$. (b) Each interior angle is $160^\circ$. (c) The interior angles add to $1800^\circ$. How many sides does each polygon have?
@step (a) $n = 360^\circ \div 24^\circ = 15$
@step (b) Exterior angle: $180^\circ - 160^\circ = 20^\circ$, so $n = 360^\circ \div 20^\circ = 18$.
@step (c) $180(n - 2) = 1800$, so $n - 2 = 10$ and $n = 12$.
@answer (a) $15$ sides; (b) $18$ sides; (c) $12$ sides
:::

:::tip Check that n is a whole number
A regular polygon must have a whole number of sides. If $360^\circ$ divided by your exterior angle is not a whole number, recheck your work: no regular polygon has that angle.
:::

:::summary
- Interior angle sum of an $n$-sided polygon: $180^\circ(n - 2)$.
- Each interior angle of a regular polygon: $\dfrac{180^\circ(n - 2)}{n}$.
- Exterior angles add to $360^\circ$; each exterior angle of a regular polygon is $\dfrac{360^\circ}{n}$.
- Interior angle $+$ exterior angle $= 180^\circ$.
- Number of sides: $n = 360^\circ \div$ (exterior angle).
:::
